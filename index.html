About these figures

Four AI weather models (FourCastNet 3, Aurora 1.5, Pangu-Weather, FengWu) are verified against the ECMWF ensemble (ENS) and its control on 133 monsoon low-pressure systems of 2018–2025. Reference tracks are found in ERA5 with the algorithm of Vishnu et al. (2020), and the same tracker is applied to every forecast. Each forecast was started 24, 48, 72 or 120 h before the genesis of the reference storm (the genesis lead). Shaded bands and error bars are 95% intervals from 1000 bootstrap replicates that resample whole storms. Click any figure to enlarge it; every file can be downloaded. The folder structure of this site mirrors the sections below.
